1. Program Description

The Z SMOKE SHOP order notification program sends transactional text messages about a customer's own pickup order. It does not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. What Messages You Will Receive

  • Order Confirmation: confirmation that your order was received
  • Order Status Updates: when your order is confirmed and being prepared
  • Ready for Pickup: when your order is ready (with the pickup window)
  • Item Availability: if an item is unavailable, a message asking how you would like to proceed
  • Reminders: a courtesy reminder about a pending pickup

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies; usually up to 8 messages per order. We send messages only between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM Central Time.

4. How to Opt In

Consent is optional and is never required to buy anything. Customers opt in only by checking an unchecked box next to this exact statement:

CONSENT STATEMENT: "I agree to receive automated text messages from Z SMOKE SHOP about this order at the phone number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

Each order requires a new opt-in. We keep a record of when consent was given.

5. How to Opt Out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT) to any message, or by calling a store. You will receive one final message confirming you are opted out, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

7. Cost & Charges

Message and data rates may apply. Z SMOKE SHOP does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier may charge standard messaging rates under your plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Privacy

Your phone number and messages are covered by our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your phone number or opt-in consent with third parties for marketing purposes. For the paused order program, your number is used only for messages about your order. For a product request, your number is used only for manual follow-up texts about that request, if you agreed to them.

9. Eligibility

  • You must be 21 years or older.
  • You must be the owner or authorized user of the phone number provided.
  • We may change or end the program at any time; we will update this page and the date above.

10. Service Provider (paused order program)

Messages in the paused automated order-notification program were delivered through Twilio Inc., a third-party communications provider.

11. Manual texts about a product request

If you submit a product request on our website and check the box agreeing to a manual text, a staff member may text you from our store phone, (512) 227-9820, about that request only (for example, to say we are looking for the item, it is ready for in-store pickup, or we could not get it).

These messages are sent by a person from the store phone. They are not automated marketing or order-status texts, and they are not sent through Twilio or another automated texting service. Consent is optional and is never required to visit the store or to buy anything in person. This is not an online sale.
